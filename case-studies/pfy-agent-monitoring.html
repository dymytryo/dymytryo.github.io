Case study

PFY agent performance management

Pay-for-you agents contact vendors and process virtual card payments on customers’ behalf, and judging their performance meant a supervisor stitching six systems together by hand every review cycle. I built the platform that did it once, governed: QuickBase, Five9, proprietary payment tables, Marqeta and Adyen reconciliation, and Workday integrated through webhooks, AWS Glue, Snowflake, and dbt, into a Tableau dashboard whose signature move was checking whether a recorded PFY attempt actually had a call behind it.
