Case study

Primary layer: one model per entity

Teams kept rebuilding the same business entities from the same sources, with slightly different answers, and every rebuild made the daily production run longer. The fix was structural: a governed primary layer between staging and everything else, one centrally owned model per entity, with the adoption of that layer measured daily until the migration actually happened. Architecture by mechanism, not memo.
