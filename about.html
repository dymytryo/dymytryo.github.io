About

Dmytro Valiaiev

I’m a data engineer, currently Staff Data Engineer / Data Architect at BILL, where I’ve grown from BI analyst to platform architect over five years. I design and migrate large-scale data platforms — and the modeling, orchestration, and governance that keep them trustworthy.

My recent work centers on lakehouse migrations: moving hundreds of terabytes off Redshift and Snowflake onto Trino + Apache Iceberg with automated validation, alongside dbt at scale, Airflow/MWAA orchestration, and CI/CD governance for thousands of SQL queries. I care about platforms that are cheaper to run and easier to trust.

Alongside engineering, I teach computer science and information systems — Python, statistics, and cloud computing — to college students, and I’m pursuing a PhD in Computer & Information Sciences.
